Know about history of chill guy.

Created by artist Phillip Banks in October 2023, the "Chill Guy" character became a global internet phenomenon by late 2024, evolving from a simple, relatable sketch of an unbothered anthropomorphic dog into a viral symbol of stress-free living. While it initially gained traction on TikTok through slideshows and "chill" edits that focused on self-care and mental health, its popularity exploded in November 2024 when unauthorized creators launched a $CHILLGUY meme coin on the Solana network, briefly pushing its market cap to $580 million with help from high-profile endorsements. This rapid commercialization—including widespread use by major corporations like Adidas and Uber Eats—triggered a legal pushback from Banks, who copyrighted the work and took action against unauthorized crypto projects, an act that led to his doxing and a subsequent drop in the coin's value. Despite this turbulence, the character solidified its place in internet history as a top-tier 2024 meme, leaving a lasting legacy that influenced music, professional sports, and pop-up retail culture.
